## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the record. Acetazolamide is known to inhibit carbonic anhydrase. It has also been used in periodic paralysis and other channelopathies.

That use suggests a possible link to pH and ion handling in muscle, which could relate to exercise-induced malignant hyperthermia. **This link is speculative.** No trial or publication was retrieved to support it, and the score alone does not show that the drug works for this condition.

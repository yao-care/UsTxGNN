# Zanubrutinib: From Mantle Cell Lymphoma to Myeloid Leukemia

## One-Sentence Summary

Zanubrutinib is a BTK inhibitor originally used for B-cell malignancies such as mantle cell lymphoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **myeloid leukemia**,
but the data compiled for this report show only **1 early-phase clinical trial** (where zanubrutinib is a combination partner) and **no myeloid-specific publications**, so the evidence is weak.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Zanubrutinib is known as a next-generation, highly selective BTK inhibitor, and its efficacy in B-cell malignancies is supported by the literature, including CLL/SLL and Waldenström macroglobulinemia.

The link to myeloid leukemia is only indirect. BTK is expressed in myeloid lineage cells and contributes to signaling independent of the B-cell receptor, so shared pathway biology could make the drug relevant. However, all supplied literature concerns B-cell malignancies, not myeloid leukemia. The high TxGNN score is a computational prediction and is not backed by myeloid-specific clinical data.

The other predicted indications (vertebral anomalies with endocrine and T-cell dysfunction, ganglioneuroblastoma, retroperitoneal neoplasm, Ewing sarcoma, neuroblastoma) have no supporting trials or meaningful literature and appear to be knowledge-graph artifacts.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified in the data compiled for this report. Examples of **Major** interactions include aprepitant, acetylsalicylic acid, dexamethasone, clarithromycin, cobicistat, deferasirox, deferiprone, samarium (153Sm) lexidronam, ibritumomab tiuxetan and tositumomab (I-131). Examples of **Moderate** interactions include cimetidine, miconazole, clotrimazole, metreleptin, fenfluramine and sibutramine.
- **Literature-noted risks**: Hepatitis B virus reactivation and hypogammaglobulinemia with increased infection risk have been described for BTK inhibitors.

For other safety information, please refer to the package insert.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests mainly on a high model score. The only trial is an early Phase 1 study in which zanubrutinib is a combination partner, and all supporting literature concerns B-cell malignancies rather than myeloid leukemia. Both US NDAs are also listed as cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data and a mechanistic rationale for BTK dependence in myeloid leukemia
- Preclinical data in myeloid leukemia models
- Results or subgroup data from NCT05665530 relevant to myeloid disease
- Package insert warnings and contraindications for safety screening
- Clarification of the cancelled US NDA status and the approved indication text

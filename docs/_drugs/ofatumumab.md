## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. The following is inferred from the known drug class, not from the supplied record. Ofatumumab is a fully human anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody. It binds a membrane-proximal epitope distinct from the one rituximab binds. Its activity is thought to come from complement-dependent cytotoxicity and antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, which may help in rituximab-refractory disease.

Follicular lymphoma is a CD20-positive B-cell malignancy, so the drug's target is expressed directly on the tumor cells. This is consistent with the high TxGNN score.

The prediction score comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Several completed Phase 2 trials, including randomized studies, directly study ofatumumab in follicular lymphoma, and the target is expressed on the tumor cells. No completed Phase 3 trial supports the indication; the one Phase 3 trial was terminated. The evidence level is therefore L2. Single-agent response rates in rituximab-refractory disease were modest.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Review of the Phase 3 trial NCT01077518 results and the reason it was terminated
- Package insert warnings and contraindications, which are missing from the data compiled for this report
- Confirmed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- A drug-interaction and infection-risk management plan, including the immunosuppressive combinations listed above
- Confirmation of the approved indications for the BLA125326 product, since no indication text is on record

The other predictions have weaker support. Metastatic neoplasm has only a 10-patient melanoma pilot and is rated Hold. Malignant spiradenoma and Langerhans cell histiocytosis have no trials or literature (evidence level L5) and are also rated Hold.

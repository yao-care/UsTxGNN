# Cyanocobalamin: From Vitamin B12 Therapy to Biotin Metabolic Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Cyanocobalamin is the injectable form of vitamin B12 and is marketed in the US as several generic products.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **biotin metabolic disease**. The 15 registered trials and 20 publications retrieved are only indirectly related, and none shows cyanocobalamin treating a primary biotin defect.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Cyanocobalamin is a form of vitamin B12 (cobalamin). The US labels retrieved contain no approved-indication text, so the original indication cannot be quoted from the data.

The link to biotin metabolic disease is indirect and sits at the pathway level. Biotin-dependent carboxylases, such as propionyl-CoA carboxylase, feed into the cobalamin-dependent methylmalonyl-CoA mutase step. Both vitamins therefore lie on the same propionate and branched-chain amino acid breakdown route. Reviews also group cobalamin and biotin together as "vitamin-responsive disorders."

The standard treatment for biotin-related disease (biotinidase deficiency, holocarboxylase synthetase deficiency) is biotin itself. No source retrieved shows cyanocobalamin correcting a primary biotin metabolic defect. The very high TxGNN score most likely reflects the two vitamins sitting close together in the knowledge graph, not a demonstrated therapeutic effect.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The high TxGNN score is not backed by direct evidence. Biotin-related disease is treated with biotin, and no trial or publication shows cyanocobalamin correcting a biotin metabolic defect. The evidence is limited to pathway-level links and general vitamin reviews (L4).

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- FDA package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Expert review of whether B12 has any role in biotinidase or holocarboxylase synthetase deficiency beyond standard biotin therapy
- Any direct preclinical or clinical study of cyanocobalamin in biotin metabolic disease
- Confirmation of the original approved indications from the US labels

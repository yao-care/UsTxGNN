## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not currently available. Based on general pharmacological knowledge, camphor modulates TRPM8 and TRPV1 channels, which produce topical cooling and counter-irritant effects. These channels are involved in sensory and pain signalling, and could plausibly relate to trigeminovascular pain pathways implicated in migraine.

This link is speculative. The retrieved publications concern cluster headache, not migraine, and in those reports camphor-containing essential oils in toothpaste were temporally associated with *triggering* headaches rather than relieving them. They do not show that camphor treats migraine. The high TxGNN score is a computational prediction only.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information. No drug interaction records were found in the data compiled for this report. Systemic exposure to camphor is associated with neurotoxicity and seizure risk, and the cited case reports describe headache episodes associated with camphor-containing essential oils.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
No clinical trial or publication directly studies camphor for migraine disorder, so the prediction rests on the model score and an unproven TRP-channel hypothesis. The available reports even suggest camphor-containing products may provoke headaches.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the FDA-approved prescribing information for aripiprazole lauroxil: "Aripiprazole lauroxil is a prodrug of aripiprazole. Following intramuscular injection, aripiprazole lauroxil is likely converted by enzyme-mediated hydrolysis to N-hydroxymethyl aripiprazole, which is then hydrolyzed to aripiprazole." ([DailyMed: Aristada (aripiprazole lauroxil) prescribing information, Alkermes, Inc.](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=17a8d11b-73b0-4833-a0b4-cf1ef85edefb), label version 2025-01-28; accessed 2026-11-19.) Based on the parent compound, aripiprazole is a dopamine D2 and serotonin 5-HT1A partial agonist and a 5-HT2A antagonist.

No mechanistic link to this prediction has been established. Inherited retinal dystrophies are typically driven by single-gene defects in photoreceptors or the retinal pigment epithelium. Dopaminergic or serotonergic modulation is not known to correct such defects. The high TxGNN score (0.9999) is a computational prediction only, and it is not supported by any trial or published study in the data compiled for this report.

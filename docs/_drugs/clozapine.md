# Clozapine: From Treatment-Resistant Schizophrenia to Manic Bipolar Affective Disorder

## One-Sentence Summary

Clozapine is an atypical antipsychotic, marketed in the US as generic tablets and orally disintegrating tablets. The license data do not list an indication, but the evidence pack ties its established use to treatment-resistant schizophrenia.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **manic bipolar affective disorder**, with **6 registered clinical trials** and **20 publications** retrieved. Only one trial is a direct clozapine efficacy study (Phase 2, double-blind, completed).

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Based on general pharmacology, clozapine blocks dopamine D2 and serotonin 5-HT2A receptors and also acts on muscarinic, adrenergic and histaminergic receptors. This broad profile underlies its established superiority in treatment-resistant schizophrenia.

Treatment-resistant mania and schizophrenia are both severe psychiatric illnesses in which first-line agents often fail. Clozapine's receptor profile plausibly supports antimanic effects and the reported reduction in suicidality in refractory illness.

Support comes from a 2020 systematic review and meta-analysis of clozapine in bipolar disorder, a 2015 systematic review in treatment-resistant bipolar disorder, and a completed Phase 2 double-blind trial in treatment-resistant mania. No randomized outcome data are included in the pack, so the evidence is promising but not definitive.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Clozapine is already marketed, and the pack scores this indication L2. A completed Phase 2 double-blind trial and systematic reviews and meta-analyses support use in treatment-refractory mania. No completed Phase 3 evidence for mania is in the pack, and clozapine carries serious known risks, so any further work should be limited to treatment-refractory patients.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking data gap), obtained from the FDA label
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Outcome data from NCT00029458 and from the Phase 4 randomized comparison NCT02562287, whose status is unknown
- Safety guardrails in any protocol: mandatory absolute neutrophil count (ANC) monitoring for agranulocytosis, and surveillance for myocarditis, seizures, metabolic effects and constipation
- Confirmation of the approved indication text for the US licenses

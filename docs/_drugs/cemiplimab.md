## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Cemiplimab is an anti-PD-1 antibody, so it blocks the PD-1 immune checkpoint and may restore T-cell activity against tumours. This class of drugs is generally used for cancers that respond to immune checkpoint blockade.

Gallbladder adenosquamous carcinoma is a rare biliary tumour. The link to cemiplimab rests only on the TxGNN prediction score. No trial or publication was supplied, and the similarity to the original indication has not been assessed. The prediction is a hypothesis for follow-up, not evidence of efficacy.

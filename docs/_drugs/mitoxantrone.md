## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Mitoxantrone is a topoisomerase II inhibitor and DNA intercalator. It kills rapidly dividing cells by damaging their DNA. A structured mechanism-of-action entry was not available in the data compiled for this report, but the literature describes it as an anthraquinone with a mechanism similar to the anthracyclines such as doxorubicin.

The original indications are all forms of acute myeloid leukemia. Upper aerodigestive tract tumors include head and neck, nasopharyngeal, salivary gland and related cancers. These are a different tumor type, but many are squamous carcinomas that respond to cytotoxic drugs. Several older phase II studies tested mitoxantrone in head and neck, nasopharyngeal and salivary gland cancers. Their results suggest only modest activity. One Southwest Oncology Group study (22 evaluable patients) reported one partial response, and the main toxicities were leukopenia and thrombocytopenia.

No clinical trials are registered under this exact disease term. The nearest registered work is under "head and neck cancer", including a completed phase Ib study of liposomal mitoxantrone and several ongoing nasopharyngeal carcinoma trials. These are not counted as evidence for this prediction.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The evidence for this specific term is limited to older single-arm phase II studies, small series and reviews, with no registered trials. These studies showed modest activity and significant myelosuppression. Newer liposomal mitoxantrone trials in head and neck and nasopharyngeal cancer are the more promising line of evidence, but they are registered under a different disease term and have not reported efficacy results.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Official package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking data gap for safety screening)
- Structured mechanism-of-action data
- Results from the ongoing liposomal mitoxantrone trials in head and neck and nasopharyngeal cancer
- A decision on the formulation to evaluate (conventional vs. liposomal) and a cardiac and hematologic monitoring plan

# Emtricitabine: From HIV Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Emtricitabine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor originally used to treat HIV infection and AIDS.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**,
but there are **0 registered clinical trials** and **20 publications** (almost all preclinical animal studies), so this is essentially an animal-model use rather than a new human indication.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, emtricitabine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NRTI) with established use in HIV, and mechanistically it may be applicable to SIV.

SIV is a close relative of HIV, and both are lentiviruses with a homologous reverse transcriptase. This is why the model gives such a high score. Macaque studies of emtricitabine combined with tenofovir (TDF or TAF) show protection against SIV/SHIV challenge. A study of the M184V mutation in SIV reverse transcriptase also points to the same drug target.

However, SIV does not infect humans. These findings mainly support the original HIV indication and the pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) rationale, not a new human therapeutic use.

Two other predictions were also reviewed:
- **Feline acquired immunodeficiency syndrome (rank 2):** FIV is also a lentivirus, so NRTI activity is plausible. Only one veterinary study exists, so it is not relevant to human repurposing (evidence L4, Hold).
- **Neurodevelopmental disorder with ataxic gait, absent speech, and decreased cortical white matter (rank 3):** No clear mechanistic link and no trials or literature. This is a computational prediction only (evidence L5, Hold).

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease-related cautions (from the knowledge graph, not label text):** Associations include liver failure, liver disease, kidney disease, lactic acidosis, Fanconi renotubular syndrome, fatty liver disease, non-alcoholic steatohepatitis, chronic hepatitis B and C virus infection, hypophosphatemia, osteomalacia, and long QT syndrome/torsades de pointes. These should be verified against the package insert.
- **Drug Interactions (20 recorded):**
  - **Major:** Leflunomide, Teriflunomide
  - **Moderate:** Orlistat, Naltrexone, Cobicistat, Methotrexate, Cladribine, Clofarabine, Epirubicin, Idelalisib, Interferon beta-1a/1b, Peginterferon beta-1a, Asparaginase, Pegaspargase, Brentuximab vedotin, Cabozantinib, Tioguanine, Trabectedin
  - **Minor:** Eribulin

For label warnings and contraindications, please refer to the package insert.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The high TxGNN score reflects the biological similarity between HIV and SIV. The supporting evidence is entirely preclinical (macaque models) and supports the existing HIV indication and PrEP use rather than a new human therapy. SIV does not infect humans, there are no registered trials, and all US authorizations are cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirmation that a human-relevant new indication exists (SIV and FIV are animal diseases)
- Mechanism of action data
- FDA package insert warnings and contraindications
- Clarification of the cancelled US market status

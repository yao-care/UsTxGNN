# Bumetanide: From Congestive Heart Failure to Acute Pulmonary Heart Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Bumetanide is a loop diuretic originally used to treat edema associated with congestive heart failure.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **acute pulmonary heart disease** (acute cor pulmonale),
but there are currently **no registered clinical trials** and only **5 publications**, none of which directly study this condition.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, bumetanide is a loop diuretic that inhibits the Na-K-2Cl cotransporter (NKCC2) in the thick ascending limb of the loop of Henle. This produces rapid natriuresis and diuresis.

In acute pulmonary heart disease, the right ventricle is overloaded and venous congestion develops. Reducing preload and intravascular volume could relieve this congestion. Published hemodynamic work in acute and chronic left heart failure shows that bumetanide reduces preload and left ventricular volume, which offers an indirect and plausible link to right-sided congestion.

There is an important caveat. The mechanism is not specific to acute cor pulmonale. Over-diuresis can lower right ventricular preload and cardiac output in acute right ventricular failure, for example in massive pulmonary embolism. Any benefit therefore depends on the patient's volume status.

## Safety Considerations

**Contraindication-related diseases (from knowledge graph, not label text):** liver disease, gallbladder disease, thrombocytopenia, potassium deficiency, diabetes mellitus, diabetic ketoacidosis, hyperuricemia, hepatic coma, hepatic encephalopathy, anuria, hypercalcemia, nephrocalcinosis, phosphorus metabolism disease, hearing disorder, and auditory system disease.

**Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were identified. The 20 reviewed were mostly Moderate, with a few Minor. Main examples:
- Antidiabetic agents (acarbose, metformin, alogliptin, albiglutide, acetohexamide)
- Corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, triamcinolone, beclomethasone)
- Amphotericin B formulations
- Proton pump inhibitors (omeprazole, rabeprazole)
- Bupropion, morphine, and bisacodyl
- Minor: doxycycline and acetylsalicylic acid

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is high, but the evidence is limited to model prediction, indirect heart failure literature, and general reviews. No trials target acute pulmonary heart disease. The mechanism could be harmful in acute right ventricular failure if volume status is not carefully managed. All US authorizations are cancelled, and label safety data are missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications (FDA)
- Mechanism of action data (DrugBank)
- Literature or studies directly addressing diuretic use in acute cor pulmonale, including volume-status criteria
- Clarification of the cancelled US regulatory status and available routes of administration
- A safety plan covering electrolyte disturbance, ototoxicity, and the interactions noted above

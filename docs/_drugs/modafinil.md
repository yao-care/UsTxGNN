# Modafinil: From Excessive Sleepiness to Insomnia

## One-Sentence Summary

Modafinil is a wake-promoting agent marketed in the US as an oral tablet, used in the literature for excessive sleepiness in narcolepsy, obstructive sleep apnea and shift work disorder.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **insomnia (disease)**, but this is most likely a knowledge-graph artifact.
Of **29 clinical trials** and **19 publications** retrieved, none shows that modafinil improves insomnia itself.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Published reviews describe modafinil as a wake-promoting agent that is pharmacologically different from other stimulants. Its efficacy in excessive sleepiness disorders is established.

The high TxGNN score most likely reflects knowledge-graph proximity between modafinil and sleep-wake disorders such as narcolepsy, sleep apnea and shift work disorder. It does not reflect a therapeutic rationale for insomnia.

Mechanistically, the direction looks opposite. Insomnia treatment needs sleep promotion, while modafinil promotes wakefulness. The analysis also notes that insomnia is a labeled adverse effect, although the package insert was not retrieved to confirm this. The retrieved records mostly address fatigue or sleepiness in comorbid conditions, not insomnia. Treat this prediction as a false-positive signal until shown otherwise.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The high TxGNN score most likely reflects the link between modafinil and sleep-wake disorders, not a treatment effect in insomnia. Modafinil is a wake-promoting drug, and the retrieved trials and literature show no evidence of benefit for insomnia. Evidence is at L4.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications, which are a blocking gap for safety screening
- Mechanism of action data, to test whether any mechanistic link to insomnia exists
- Results of NCT00124384 (modafinil in primary insomnia, Phase 4), the only directly relevant modafinil trial
- Full records for trials whose titles are truncated or whose relevance is still pending, to confirm target conditions
- Clarification of which RECOVER-SLEEP arms include modafinil
- A check that the prediction is not a false positive, for example by testing whether the intended target is a sleep-wake disorder or a daytime symptom rather than insomnia

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, acitretin is a second-generation oral retinoid. It normalizes keratinocyte differentiation and proliferation and reduces follicular hyperkeratinization. That gives a plausible class-level link to disorders of the pilosebaceous unit.

The relationship to the predicted indication needs care. Acitretin has weaker direct sebosuppressive activity than isotretinoin, and it is not a standard therapy for acne vulgaris. The strongest signal in the retrieved data concerns acne inversa (hidradenitis suppurativa), a follicular occlusion disorder. The "acne (disease)" label appears to blend the two conditions. The high TxGNN score (99.94%) should therefore be interpreted cautiously.

## Safety Considerations

- **Teratogenicity**: Acitretin is a potent teratogen, so strict pregnancy avoidance is required. The washout is prolonged because etretinate can form in the presence of alcohol.
- **Contraindication signals from the knowledge graph** (not label text): liver failure, drug-induced liver injury, pancreatitis, hypertriglyceridemia, diabetes mellitus, diabetic ketoacidosis, pseudotumor cerebri/intracranial hypertension, vision disorders including night blindness, hyperostosis, obesity disorders, substance abuse/dependence, and anxiety, neurotic and dysthymic disorders.
- **Drug interactions** (100 identified in total; main ones listed):
  - **Major**: tetracycline, doxycycline, minocycline, vitamin A
  - **Moderate**: sulfonylurea antidiabetics (acetohexamide, chlorpropamide, glimepiride, glipizide, glyburide, tolazamide, tolbutamide) and naltrexone

Please refer to the package insert for full warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only clinical trial concerns hidradenitis suppurativa, not acne vulgaris, and it is a Phase 4 comparison with no results. The other support consists of reviews, guidelines and case reports. Acitretin is not a standard acne therapy, carries serious teratogenicity risk, and currently has no valid US license. The high TxGNN score likely reflects blending of acne vulgaris and acne inversa.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Clarify whether the target is acne vulgaris or hidradenitis suppurativa, and refine the disease mapping.
- Obtain results from NCT07708480 and look for controlled studies of acitretin in the chosen indication.
- Obtain the FDA package insert warnings and contraindications, and the mechanism of action data.
- Define a pregnancy-prevention and risk-management plan, with monitoring of liver function and lipids.

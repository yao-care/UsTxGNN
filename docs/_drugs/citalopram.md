## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Citalopram is an SSRI. Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report, but the SSRI class mechanism is well established: it blocks serotonin reuptake and increases serotonergic signaling.

Serotonergic dysfunction is central to the pathophysiology of obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), and serotonin reuptake inhibitors are a first-line drug treatment for it. A drug in this class is therefore mechanistically plausible for OCD, which is consistent with the high TxGNN score.

Citalopram-specific support comes from small open-label and pilot studies in adults, treatment-resistant patients, and children and adolescents. The trials listed below are largely class-level (SSRIs in general, or fluoxetine) rather than citalopram-specific, so the case rests on the SSRI class plus preliminary citalopram data.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Citalopram is an SSRI, a class widely used in OCD, and several small citalopram studies in OCD point the same way. However, no citalopram-specific randomized controlled trial in OCD is included, so the evidence is indirect for the drug itself. Under the evidence rules, the level is therefore capped at L4 rather than the higher level suggested by class-level data.

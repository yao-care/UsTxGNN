## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Esketamine is an NMDA receptor antagonist that produces rapid glutamatergic and synaptic plasticity effects. These effects have been studied in treatment-resistant depression and, in smaller exploratory work, in anxiety-spectrum conditions.

The link to agoraphobia is indirect. It is extrapolated from the general anxiety-disorder literature, and there is no agoraphobia-specific mechanism or data. The rationale rests on general knowledge of the drug class, not on evidence specific to this drug and condition.

The TxGNN score of 99.57% is a knowledge-graph computational prediction. It is not clinical evidence and should be treated as a hypothesis-generating signal only.

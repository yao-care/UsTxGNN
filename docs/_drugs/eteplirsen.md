## One-Sentence Summary

Eteplirsen is an antisense oligonucleotide originally used to treat Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) in patients whose mutations are amenable to exon 51 skipping.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Eteplirsen is a phosphorodiamidate morpholino oligomer (PMO) antisense drug. It induces skipping of exon 51 in DMD pre-mRNA, which restores the reading frame. The result is a shortened but partly functional dystrophin protein in patients with exon 51-amenable deletions. The data compiled for this report do not include a structured mechanism-of-action entry, but this mechanism is well described in the published literature.

The predicted indication is not a true repurposing candidate. It is essentially the drug's established use, so the prediction agrees with known biology. Two caveats apply:

- The benefit is limited to exon 51-skippable genotypes, roughly 13% of DMD patients.
- The pivotal evidence relied on dystrophin expression as a surrogate endpoint (accelerated approval). Clinical benefit is still being confirmed in an ongoing Phase 3 trial.

The other nine predictions (ranks 2-10) are conditions such as Bethlem myopathy, Emery-Dreifuss muscular dystrophy and limb-girdle dystrophies. They have different genetic causes, and eteplirsen's exon 51 skipping has no plausible mechanism there. The high scores most likely reflect shared muscular dystrophy network associations. No trials or literature were found for any of them, so they are not recommended for pursuit.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
The top prediction (DMD) is the drug's established indication, backed by one completed Phase 3 open-label controlled study and an ongoing randomized Phase 3 trial. Strictly, the evidence level is closer to L2 than L1. The criterion for L1 (at least two completed Phase 3 RCTs) is not met, because the completed Phase 3 study is open-label and non-randomized and the randomized Phase 3 trial is still in progress. Confirmatory clinical benefit has not yet been demonstrated. This is a confirmation of existing use rather than new repurposing. The other nine predictions have no evidence and no mechanistic basis, so they should stay on Hold.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Safety information from the current package insert, because the warnings and contraindications data are missing. This is a blocking gap for safety screening.
- Structured mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank.
- Results from the Phase 3 high-dose trial (NCT03992430), expected to complete around October 2026, to confirm clinical benefit.
- Clarification of the cancelled NDA206488 status and the current US marketing position.
- Genotype restriction (exon 51-amenable mutations) built into any further development plan.

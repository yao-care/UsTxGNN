## Why Is This Prediction Reasonable? (Or Unreasonable?)

However, the analysis text from another item in the candidate list (ranked 9th) provides credible supplementary information: Nafarelin is a GnRH agonist; chronic administration causes desensitization of pituitary GnRH receptors, suppressing LH/FSH secretion, and thereby reducing gonadal steroid concentrations—this is indeed the known mechanism for its use in central precocious puberty and endometriosis.

However, for this page's top-ranked prediction "Ambras type hypertrichosis universalis congenita" (a congenital hypertrichosis syndrome), the mechanistic association analysis in the evidence bundle itself clearly states: **"No mechanistic association.

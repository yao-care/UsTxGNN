## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Adenine is a purine base that takes part in nucleotide metabolism and adenosine signalling, but its established therapeutic mechanism and original indication cannot be confirmed from the data compiled for this report.

One speculative, indirect hypothesis is that adenosine receptors (e.g., A2A) have been reported in preclinical work to influence osteoblast and osteoclast activity. This could in theory connect purine biology to bone metabolism. This link is not supported by any data in this report, and the high TxGNN score reflects knowledge-graph proximity only.

There is also a safety signal pointing the opposite way. At high doses, adenine is used to induce chronic kidney disease in rodent models, and chronic kidney disease can itself cause mineral and bone disorder. This is a potential harm rather than a benefit signal.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has a high model score but no supporting clinical trials, no directly relevant literature, no mechanism data, and no active US licence. The evidence is at L5 (model prediction only). **To proceed, the following is needed:**

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Brivaracetam is generally understood to act by binding SV2A, a protein on synaptic vesicles, which modulates neurotransmitter release and reduces neuronal hyperexcitability.

Beta-ketothiolase deficiency is an inherited metabolic disorder of ketone body and isoleucine breakdown. Its neurological problems, including seizures, arise mainly as a consequence of ketoacidotic crises. Brivaracetam's mechanism does not correct the underlying enzyme defect, so the mechanistic link is weak. The high TxGNN score is a model output and is not supported by any trial or published study in the compiled data.

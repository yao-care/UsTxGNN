## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for clarithromycin are not available in the data compiled for this report. Clarithromycin is a macrolide antibiotic that is known to have some immunomodulatory properties.

Polyclonal hyperviscosity syndrome is a condition in which raised levels of immunoglobulins from many B-cell clones thicken the blood. No established link was identified between clarithromycin and this mechanism. The high score is a computational prediction only and is not backed by any biological or clinical rationale found so far. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis, not as a reasoned therapeutic direction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. From general pharmacology, hydroquinone inhibits tyrosinase and reduces melanin synthesis. This is why it is used in pigmentary conditions such as melasma.

Seborrheic keratosis is a benign epidermal growth that is often pigmented. A depigmenting agent might lighten the colour of these lesions, but it would not treat the growth itself. The only supporting document is a prospective observational study of combination treatment for facial pigmentary disorders, which is indirect evidence. The high TxGNN score is a computational prediction and should not be read as clinical proof.

Other predicted indications are weaker still:
- **Vulvar inverted follicular keratosis** (score 99.64%): no trials or literature, and no clear mechanistic rationale. The prediction is likely driven by network proximity to other keratosis conditions.
- **Exanthem** (score 99.42%): a broad rash category with no mechanistic link to depigmentation. The one related trial tested an herbal depigmenting system for pigmentation.
- **Lichen disease** (score 99.07%): hydroquinone could only address post-inflammatory pigmentation, not the underlying inflammation.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is high, but there are no clinical trials for seborrheic keratosis. The only literature is indirect, and hydroquinone could at most lighten pigmentation rather than treat the lesion. The US license is cancelled, and safety data from the package insert are missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- FDA package insert warnings and contraindications, to complete the safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Direct clinical evidence of hydroquinone in seborrheic keratosis, such as a small controlled study
- Review of the knowledge-graph contraindication signals, especially for dermatitis and skin atrophy, which are relevant to topical use
- Clarification of the regulatory status, since the only US license on record is cancelled

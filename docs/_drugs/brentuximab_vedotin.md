# Brentuximab Vedotin: From Hodgkin Lymphoma and Anaplastic Large Cell Lymphoma to Follicular Lymphoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Brentuximab vedotin (ADCETRIS) is an anti-CD30 antibody-drug conjugate. Published literature describes it as approved for CD30-positive lymphomas, chiefly Hodgkin lymphoma and systemic anaplastic large cell lymphoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **follicular lymphoma**, but only **6 clinical trials** (3 withdrawn, 1 recruiting single-arm) and **20 publications** were retrieved, and none shows follicular-lymphoma-specific efficacy.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Brentuximab vedotin (BV) is an anti-CD30 antibody-drug conjugate. It delivers MMAE, a microtubule-disrupting agent, to CD30-positive cells. A structured mechanism-of-action entry was not available for this drug, so the description above comes from the mechanistic notes in the evidence pack.

Follicular lymphoma is a B-cell lymphoma, and BV is already used in other CD30-expressing lymphomas. However, CD30 is not a defining marker of follicular lymphoma. The link therefore depends on CD30-positive subsets or on transformed disease. The literature includes a case of grade I follicular lymphoma transformed to CD30+ anaplastic large cell lymphoma that responded completely to BV plus high-dose methotrexate. This is a single case and does not show activity in typical follicular lymphoma.

The very high TxGNN score is a model prediction, not clinical evidence. Most of the supplied literature concerns peripheral T-cell lymphoma rather than follicular lymphoma.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Follicular lymphoma is not a CD30-defined disease. The only on-indication trial is a small single-arm study with no results, and three of the six listed trials were withdrawn. The one randomized trial was terminated early. The supporting literature is mostly about peripheral T-cell lymphoma, and the very high TxGNN score is not clinical evidence.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Results from NCT04587687 (BV + bendamustine in relapsed/refractory follicular lymphoma) and any data from the terminated NCT02594163
- CD30 expression prevalence data in follicular lymphoma, including transformed disease, to define a target subgroup
- The current US package insert (indications, warnings, contraindications) and the drug's original indication list
- Structured mechanism-of-action data and a safety monitoring plan, with attention to neuropathy and infection risk

**Related finding:** the rank 4 prediction, B-cell neoplasm, has stronger support (L2, "Proceed with Guardrails"). It rests on multiple completed Phase 2 trials in CD30+ Hodgkin lymphoma, DLBCL and primary mediastinal B-cell lymphoma. That candidate should be verified against current labeling first, since it may be on-label.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Sodium acetate is used as an electrolyte source and alkalinizing agent. Acetate is metabolized to bicarbonate, which explains its role in fluid and acid-base management.

The mechanistic case for this prediction is weak. Congenital prothrombin deficiency is a defect in coagulation factor II, and sodium acetate has no known role in synthesizing or replacing it. The very high score (99.98%, rank 2 among this drug's predictions) reflects proximity in the knowledge graph, not a biological rationale. Treat it as a hypothesis-generating signal only.

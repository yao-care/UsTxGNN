# Bexarotene: From Cutaneous T-Cell Lymphoma to Primary Cutaneous B-Cell Lymphoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Bexarotene is an oral and topical retinoid X receptor (RXR) agonist, used mainly for cutaneous T-cell lymphoma (CTCL) according to the literature and trial records.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **primary cutaneous B-cell lymphoma**, but only **2 loosely related clinical trials** and **general cutaneous lymphoma literature** exist, with no B-cell-specific bexarotene data.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the input. From the literature, bexarotene is a selective RXR agonist that modulates gene expression, cell growth, differentiation and apoptosis. Its efficacy in CTCL is well established, and it is used both orally and topically.

The high TxGNN score most likely reflects the shared "cutaneous lymphoma" neighborhood in the knowledge graph. Primary cutaneous B-cell lymphoma and CTCL are both primary cutaneous lymphomas and appear together in many reviews.

However, the evidence that RXR activation is therapeutically relevant in B-cell lymphomas (marginal zone, follicle center, large B-cell) is lacking. The related trials involve T-cell lymphoma or do not use bexarotene, and the literature is mostly general reviews and case reports. The prediction is therefore a hypothesis, not a supported indication.

For comparison, the same evidence pack's rank 2 prediction, **Sezary syndrome** (a leukemic CTCL variant), has far stronger support. It has several completed Phase 2 bexarotene combination trials and one terminated Phase 3 trial (NCT00056056), and is scored L1.

## Safety Considerations

- **Reported in the literature**: hypertriglyceridemia (the most frequent adverse effect, often requiring dose reduction or interruption, with higher risk at higher BMI), central hypothyroidism (concomitant levothyroxine is recommended), and neutropenia with the capsules. The gel causes an irritant dermatitis.

No structured warnings, contraindications or drug interaction data were available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The 99.44% TxGNN score is not backed by bexarotene evidence in B-cell disease. The only related trials are a T-cell combination study and a withdrawn trial without bexarotene, and the literature is general reviews and case reports. Evidence is L4 (model prediction plus mechanistic plausibility only). The stronger candidate from this pack is Sezary syndrome (CTCL).

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Preclinical evidence that RXR activation affects B-cell lymphoma cells (marginal zone, follicle center, large B-cell)
- Any clinical case series or trial with bexarotene in primary cutaneous B-cell lymphoma
- The original indication and mechanism of action from the package insert or DrugBank
- FDA package insert warnings and contraindications for safety screening
- A route-of-administration comparison (oral capsule vs topical gel) against the lesion type

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for hyaluronic acid are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on its known properties, hyaluronic acid binds large amounts of water and stabilises moist surfaces, so it works as a lubricant and tear-film stabiliser.

Sjogren syndrome is an autoimmune disease that reduces tear and saliva production. Patients typically suffer from dryness of the eyes and mouth. A moisture-retaining, lubricating agent is therefore a plausible symptomatic aid.

Any benefit would be **palliative**. Hyaluronic acid would ease surface dryness but would not modify the underlying autoimmune process. Topical hyaluronic acid approaches for dry mouth, such as hyaluronate sheets, are still exploratory.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The interaction query returned 100 records. The most notable is a **major** interaction with **adalimumab**, which suggests avoiding combined use with other biologic immunomodulators. Moderate interactions include warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, dicoumarol, tacrolimus, simvastatin, theophylline, aminophylline, nifedipine, fentanyl, hydrocodone, lidocaine and roflumilast. Several zinc salts have minor interactions. Interactions with a tetanus toxoid vaccine are also listed as moderate.
- **Class effects (from the literature)**: IL-17 inhibitors have been associated with paradoxical worsening of inflammatory bowel disease, and infection risk should be monitored.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications, as these were not available in the data compiled for this report.

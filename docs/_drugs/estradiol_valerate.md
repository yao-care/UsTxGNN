## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Estradiol valerate is an estrogen ester, and estrogen replacement is an established supportive treatment for low estrogen states. Its efficacy in its labeled uses is presumed, but the source data does not list an approved indication.

The most plausible link is indirect. Women who carry a fragile X premutation can develop fragile X-associated primary ovarian insufficiency (FXPOI), and estrogen replacement is standard supportive care for the resulting hypoestrogenism. That would be a symptom-level overlap with existing estrogen use.

It would not be evidence that estradiol valerate benefits fragile X syndrome itself. The model score is very high (99.94%), but without trials or literature it should be treated as a hypothesis only.

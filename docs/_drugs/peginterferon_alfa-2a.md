## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, peginterferon alfa-2a is a pegylated form of interferon alfa. It acts through the interferon-alpha receptor and induces interferon-stimulated genes, giving it both direct antiviral and immunomodulatory effects. These effects are thought to support control of HBV replication and loss of the surface antigen (HBsAg).

Chronic hepatitis B is already listed among the drug's original indications, so the prediction mostly confirms existing use. The many HBV trials and publications on file are consistent with this.

The main caution is regulatory. The only US license on record (Pegasys, BLA103964) is cancelled, so product availability and local regulatory status must be confirmed before any use.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Peginterferon alfa-2a is an established therapy for chronic hepatitis B, with supporting RCTs, meta-analyses, and many Phase 3/4 trials. The guardrail is that the only US license is cancelled, and label-based safety data have not yet been reviewed.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirm product availability and current regulatory status, since the only US license is cancelled
- Obtain and review the package insert for warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Obtain detailed mechanism of action data
- Create an interaction management plan for the major interactions listed above

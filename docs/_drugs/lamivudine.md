## Lamivudine: From Chronic Hepatitis B and HIV Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Lamivudine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor originally used to treat chronic hepatitis B virus infection, HIV infection and AIDS.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**, but this is an animal-model (non-human) disease, supported by **0 clinical trials** and **15 publications**, mostly preclinical macaque studies or indirect reviews.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, lamivudine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NRTI). After phosphorylation inside cells, it acts as a chain terminator of viral DNA synthesis. Its efficacy in HIV and hepatitis B has been established.

SIV is a close lentiviral relative of HIV with a homologous reverse transcriptase, so lamivudine's mechanism is plausibly shared. Macaque studies support this. They include work on the M184V resistance mutation in SIV reverse transcriptase, multidrug antiretroviral regimens, and post-exposure prophylaxis with zidovudine, lamivudine and indinavir.

However, SIV is a non-human research-model disease, so this is **not a human repurposing opportunity**. Its main value is as a preclinical model for HIV. Other predictions in the same list are similarly limited:
- **Feline acquired immunodeficiency syndrome** (score 99.93%): evidence is limited to small veterinary and in vitro studies.
- **A rare neurodevelopmental disorder** (99.93%) and **obsolete familial combined hyperlipidemia** (99.63%): no mechanistic link and no supporting evidence.
- **Chronic hepatitis C** (99.11%): HCV replicates through NS5B RNA polymerase, which is not a lamivudine target, and the literature reflects HBV/HCV co-discussion rather than efficacy. Direct-acting antivirals already cure HCV.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The predicted indication, SIV infection, is a non-human research-model disease with no clinical trials and only preclinical macaque evidence (Evidence Level L4). It offers no human repurposing opportunity, and lamivudine's US licenses are all cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirmation that a human indication is actually being targeted. The current top predictions are animal-model or veterinary diseases, and the human-relevant ones are not supported.
- Official package insert warnings and contraindications, which are currently missing and block safety screening.
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank.
- Clarification of the cancelled US license status, and the current regulatory pathway if development is pursued.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Based on known information, polyethylene glycol 3500 is an osmotic laxative and a hydrophilic polymer, and it has no established mechanism in ichthyosis.

Congenital ichthyosiform erythroderma is a disorder of skin keratinization, often linked to defects in pathways such as TGM1 or ALOXE3/ALOX12B. Oral or ophthalmic PEG 3500 is not expected to act on these underlying abnormalities. A speculative link would be a topical humectant or barrier-hydration effect on hyperkeratotic skin, but nothing in the data compiled for this report supports it.

The high TxGNN score reflects a network-based computational prediction only. It should not be read as evidence of clinical benefit.

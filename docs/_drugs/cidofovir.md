## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, cidofovir is a nucleotide analog that inhibits viral DNA synthesis and is active against DNA viruses such as cytomegalovirus (CMV).

A direct link to sclerosing cholangitis, which is mainly an immune-mediated or cholestatic bile duct disease, is not evident. One possible indirect route is secondary sclerosing cholangitis or viral cholangiopathy in immunocompromised patients, for example when CMV is involved. This is speculative and has no supporting studies in the compiled data.

The very high score (0.9994) is a model output only. It should not be read as evidence of efficacy. Cidofovir's known nephrotoxicity is also a concern for any new use.

# Anastrozole: From Hormone Receptor-Positive Breast Cancer to Female Breast Carcinoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Anastrozole is a non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor used in hormone receptor-positive breast cancer in postmenopausal women.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **female breast carcinoma**, which is essentially its established use rather than a true repurposing, and this is supported by **30 registered clinical trials** and **20 publications** compiled for this report.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data was not available in the structured drug record. Based on known pharmacology, anastrozole is a third-generation non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor. It blocks the conversion of androgens to estrogens and lowers circulating estrogen in postmenopausal women. This directly targets estrogen-driven growth in hormone receptor-positive breast cancer.

The predicted indication closely matches the drug's established use, so the high score is consistent with known pharmacology. This is better read as confirmation of the model than as a new repurposing opportunity.

The other predictions, such as neuroblastoma, ganglioneuroblastoma, monocytic leukemia and a rare vertebral/endocrine syndrome, have no clinical trials or literature. They have no clear mechanistic rationale and are likely computational artifacts (Hold). Retroperitoneal neoplasm has only a single case report, which describes metastatic breast cancer treated with S-1 and not anastrozole (Hold).

## Safety Considerations

- **Knowledge-graph contraindication signals** (from a computational knowledge graph, not label text): myocardial ischemia and coronary artery disease. These should be checked against the official label.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 potential interactions were identified, but all are of unknown severity. Examples include bupropion, omeprazole, pantoprazole, metformin, glimepiride, simvastatin, morphine, prednisone and acetylsalicylic acid. Their clinical significance is not established.
- **Related study findings**: trials and publications compiled for this report examine bone density and muscle changes, endothelial function and a hepatotoxicity case report in women taking aromatase inhibitors. These are signals to monitor, not label statements.

Please refer to the package insert for the full warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Multiple completed Phase 3 trials (such as ATAC-related studies and trials against fulvestrant and exemestane) and several RCT publications support anastrozole in hormone receptor-positive breast cancer, giving an L1 evidence level. However, this is the established indication, not a new one. All US licenses are currently cancelled, and label-based safety data is missing.

# Propylhexedrine: From Nasal Decongestion to Attention Deficit-Hyperactivity Disorder

## One-Sentence Summary

Propylhexedrine is a volatile sympathomimetic amine that was marketed as an inhalant (BENZEDREX); its only U.S. license on record is now cancelled.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Propylhexedrine is a sympathomimetic amine, structurally related to amphetamine and methamphetamine (a cycloalkyl analog). A plausible link to ADHD would be through catecholamine release or reuptake modulation, which is how established ADHD stimulants work.

This link is speculative. Propylhexedrine is reported to have much weaker central activity than amphetamine, and no mechanism data were available for this report. The argument rests only on structural-class inference and a high model score (0.9997). Misuse potential at high doses is also a safety concern.

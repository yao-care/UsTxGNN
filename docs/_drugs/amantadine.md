Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Amantadine is generally known as an NMDA receptor antagonist with dopaminergic effects, and this general pharmacology is the basis for the proposed link.

Rasmussen encephalitis is a rare, immune-mediated brain disease that causes progressive neuronal injury and intractable seizures. Glutamate-related excitotoxicity is thought to contribute to the neuronal damage, so NMDA receptor blockade offers a plausible but purely theoretical rationale.

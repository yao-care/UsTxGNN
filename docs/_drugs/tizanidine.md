## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Tizanidine is a central alpha-2 adrenergic agonist. Alpha-2 agonism can reduce trigeminovascular pain signalling and central sensitization, and may lower the muscle tension that contributes to headache. This gives a plausible basis for migraine and chronic daily headache prevention. Detailed mechanism-of-action data were not available in the data compiled for this report, so this reasoning rests on the drug's known class properties.

Clinical data point in the same direction. A double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter study (PMID 12167135, 2002) tested tizanidine as add-on preventive therapy for chronic daily headache, a group in which most patients had migraine. A Phase 3 trial specifically in migraine prevention (NCT05484349) is registered, but its status is unknown and no results are available. The evidence is therefore encouraging but not yet confirmed in migraine itself.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. Major ones include famotidine, morphine, cimetidine, dolasetron and obeticholic acid. Moderate ones include hyoscyamine, loperamide, atropine, clarithromycin and levofloxacin. CYP1A2 inhibitors such as fluvoxamine and ciprofloxacin are of particular concern and are contraindicated.
- **Other cautions**: Sedation, low blood pressure and liver toxicity (liver enzyme monitoring) are recognized concerns. Conditions flagged in the drug-disease contraindication data include liver failure, kidney disease, psychotic and other mental disorders, and hypotensive disorder.

Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
A randomized placebo-controlled trial in chronic daily headache (most patients had migraine) and a registered Phase 3 migraine prevention trial support the prediction, placing it at evidence level L2. The Phase 3 trial has no reported results, so efficacy in migraine itself remains unconfirmed.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Published results or status update for NCT05484349
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- Safety monitoring plan covering sedation, hypotension, liver enzymes and CYP1A2-inhibitor interactions
- Review of the package insert warnings and contraindications

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on what is known, pazopanib is a multi-target tyrosine kinase inhibitor with anti-angiogenic and antitumour properties, acting on the PDGFR and VEGFR pathways. These pathways are relevant to soft tissue sarcoma.

Pazopanib is used in non-adipocytic soft tissue sarcoma, but liposarcoma, an adipocytic subtype, was excluded from the registration trial. Evidence in liposarcoma therefore comes from separate Phase 2 studies, including two dedicated single-agent trials (NCT01506596, NCT01692496) and a published Phase 2 report (PMID 31010343). Results are modest, and no Phase 3 trial exists.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindication-related conditions on record**: Records list hypertension, long QT syndrome and torsades de pointes, liver failure and drug-induced liver injury, hyperbilirubinemia (including Gilbert syndrome), hypothyroidism, myocardial infarction and coronary thrombosis, intracerebral hemorrhage, active peptic ulcer disease, pancreatitis, proteinuria, diarrheal disease, and several electrolyte and mineral disorders. These are not taken from label text and should be checked against the package insert.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions are recorded. Major interactions include acid-suppressing drugs (famotidine, ranitidine, cimetidine, omeprazole, esomeprazole, rabeprazole, dexlansoprazole), clarithromycin and dolasetron. Moderate interactions include pioglitazone, loperamide, aprepitant, dexamethasone, budesonide, saxagliptin, eliglustat, bisacodyl, magnesium oxide and polyethylene glycol with electrolytes.

Package insert warnings and contraindications are not included in the data compiled for this report; please refer to the package insert.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Two liposarcoma-specific Phase 2 trials (NCT01506596, NCT01692496) and a published Phase 2 report support activity, which places the evidence at L2. Results are modest, there is no Phase 3 trial, and liposarcoma was excluded from the registration trial for soft tissue sarcoma.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Review of the Phase 2 efficacy results by liposarcoma subtype (well/dedifferentiated, myxoid, pleomorphic)
- Package insert warnings and contraindications, and mechanism-of-action data
- A monitoring plan covering blood pressure, QT interval, liver function and interacting acid-suppressing drugs
- Comparison against current second-line options such as trabectedin and eribulin, ideally in a randomized study

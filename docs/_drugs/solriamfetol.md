## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Solriamfetol inhibits the reuptake of dopamine and norepinephrine. Enhancing catecholamine signaling in prefrontal circuits is the established mechanism behind ADHD drugs such as methylphenidate and atomoxetine, so the link is biologically plausible. The mechanism-of-action field in the data compiled for this report is empty, so this reasoning rests on the drug's known pharmacology rather than on the supplied data.

Its wake-promoting profile also fits the attention and alertness problems seen in ADHD. Stimulants carry risks of misuse and dependence, and a review of newer ADHD treatments discusses the interest in options beyond stimulants.

## Safety Considerations

**Drug Interactions**: 35 interactions were recorded. Examples:
- **Major**: methylene blue
- **Moderate**: sympathomimetics and related agents, including epinephrine, ephedrine, pseudoephedrine, phenylephrine, isometheptene and mazindol; and beta-agonist bronchodilators, including salbutamol, formoterol, olodaterol, vilanterol, isoprenaline, pirbuterol and orciprenaline

Because of its noradrenergic action, blood pressure and heart rate should be monitored. For other safety information, please refer to the package insert.

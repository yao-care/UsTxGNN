## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The only interaction record is pharmacological. Erenumab acts on the CGRP receptor, its intended target. No drug-drug interactions were reported.
- **Vascular safety in aura**: Patients with migraine with aura have elevated vascular risk. The supplied analyses (PMIDs 34928306 and 36942409) assessed erenumab safety in this group, but they do not cover the brainstem aura subtype.

Please refer to the package insert for other safety information.

# Clonidine: From Its US-Marketed Use to Faciodigitogenital Syndrome

## One-Sentence Summary

Clonidine is a centrally acting alpha-2 adrenergic agonist that is currently marketed in the US as injection, tablet, and patch products.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **faciodigitogenital syndrome**, a rare developmental syndrome.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Clonidine is known as an alpha-2 adrenergic agonist. This class lowers noradrenergic tone and is used for conditions involving arousal, impulsivity, and sympathetic overactivity.

The review of this prediction found no plausible mechanistic link between alpha-2 agonism and faciodigitogenital syndrome, which is a rare developmental syndrome. The similarity between the original and predicted indications has not been assessed. The very high model score is therefore not backed by any biological rationale, clinical study, or publication.

# Insulin Glulisine: From Diabetes Mellitus (Glycemic Control) to Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus

## One-Sentence Summary

Insulin glulisine (Apidra) is a rapid-acting human insulin analogue used at mealtimes to improve glycemic control in diabetes.
The TxGNN model predicts it is effective for **type 1 diabetes mellitus**, with **over 40 registered clinical trials** and **16 publications** in the evidence pack. This prediction largely confirms an existing use of the drug rather than a new repurposing finding.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the evidence pack. Insulin glulisine is a rapid-acting insulin analogue that acts on the insulin receptor to replace mealtime (prandial) insulin. Published reviews describe it as having a faster onset and shorter duration of action than regular human insulin.

Type 1 diabetes is defined by absolute insulin deficiency, so the mechanistic link is direct. The high TxGNN score (99.55%) matches what the drug is already used for.

**Guardrail:** The empty original-indication field and missing mechanism data look like gaps in the dataset, not a true repurposing signal. Treat this result as confirmation of an existing use.

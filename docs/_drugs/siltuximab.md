## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, siltuximab is an anti-IL-6 monoclonal antibody, and its efficacy in Castleman disease is linked to IL-6 driven lymphoproliferation and inflammation.

A possible rationale for extracutaneous mastocytoma is that IL-6 may play a role in mast cell proliferation and inflammatory signaling. However, no supporting trial or publication was found, so this link is speculative. The prediction should be regarded as a hypothesis generated by the model, not as an evidence-based indication.

Other predicted indications were also reviewed. All remain at the model-prediction stage (L5), except Kaposi's sarcoma (L4), which has an indirect link through HHV-8-associated Castleman disease (see the note in the conclusion).

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The interaction screen returned 100 potential interactions. Of the 20 listed in the data compiled for this report, the main ones are:
  - **Major**: Deferiprone
  - **Moderate**: Simvastatin, warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, dicoumarol, tacrolimus, theophylline, aminophylline, oxtriphylline, roflumilast, lidocaine, fentanyl, hydrocodone, nifedipine, tetanus toxoid vaccine

  Many of these are drugs with a narrow therapeutic index that are metabolised by CYP enzymes (warfarin, tacrolimus, theophylline, simvastatin). IL-6 blockade can normalise suppressed CYP activity, so drug levels should be monitored when treatment starts or stops.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests only on a model score (99.64%), with no clinical trials or publications for extracutaneous mastocytoma. The mechanistic link is speculative, mastocytosis-type diseases are driven mainly by KIT signaling, and the only US license has been cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data and a review of IL-6 involvement in mast cell disease
- Preclinical or literature evidence for IL-6 blockade in mastocytoma
- FDA package insert warnings and contraindications, which are required before safety screening
- A decision on product availability, since the Sylvant license is cancelled

**Note on other predictions:** Kaposi's sarcoma is the most promising alternative (L4, "Research Question"). It shares pathogenic features with HHV-8-associated Castleman disease, including viral and human IL-6 dysregulation. The only publication retrieved (PMID [39970477](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39970477/), a 2024 review in AIDS Reviews on Castleman disease) is indirect and does not study siltuximab in Kaposi's sarcoma. The remaining predictions (hepatic infarction, hepatic veno-occlusive disease, peliosis hepatis, familial Mediterranean fever, aggressive systemic mastocytosis, autosomal dominant familial periodic fever) have no supporting evidence and remain on Hold.

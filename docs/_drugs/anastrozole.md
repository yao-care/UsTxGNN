## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the input. Anastrozole is a non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor that lowers estrogen synthesis. Estrogen drives hormone receptor-positive breast cancer in postmenopausal women, so the link between mechanism and disease is direct.

Phase 3 RCTs cited in the literature provide direct evidence. ATAC compared anastrozole with tamoxifen, and IBIS-II tested anastrozole against placebo for prevention. Because the input lists no original indication, breast cancer is very likely an on-label use. Treat this as a validation of the model rather than a new-use discovery until the label is checked.

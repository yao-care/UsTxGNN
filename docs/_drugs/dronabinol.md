## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Dronabinol is known as a synthetic form of delta-9-THC that acts as a CB1/CB2 agonist.

The endocannabinoid system is thought to modulate trigeminovascular pain signalling, serotonergic signalling and release of CGRP (calcitonin gene-related peptide), all of which are involved in migraine. This gives a plausible mechanistic basis for the prediction.

The very high TxGNN score is a computational result only. It does not by itself show that the drug works in migraine, and no published study confirms this.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only study directly addressing migraine is a completed Phase 2 trial with no available results, and no published study tests dronabinol in migraine. Support is therefore the model prediction and a plausible mechanism only (evidence level L5).

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Results of NCT00123201 (efficacy, safety, enrollment) from the sponsor or registry
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Warnings and contraindications from the package insert
- A safety review of psychoactive effects and interactions with other central nervous system depressants in migraine patients
